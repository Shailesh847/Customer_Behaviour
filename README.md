# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 1. Business Problem

**Why do customers buy, what influences their spending, and how can a business turn one-time shoppers into loyal customers?**

Retail businesses generate large amounts of transaction data, but raw data alone cannot explain customer preferences, subscription behavior, discount dependency, or purchasing patterns.

This project analyzes customer shopping transactions to identify the factors influencing purchase behavior, compare customer segments, and uncover opportunities to improve revenue, customer retention, and marketing effectiveness.

The objective is to transform raw shopping data into actionable business insights using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

## 2. Project Overview

Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis is an end-to-end data analytics project that explores customer demographics, purchasing patterns, product preferences, discounts, shipping methods, and subscription behavior.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow, from data loading and cleaning to SQL-based business analysis, interactive dashboard development, and business recommendations.

**Key objectives:**
- Understand customer purchasing and spending patterns.
- Compare revenue across gender and age groups.
- Analyze subscriber and non-subscriber behavior.
- Identify top-rated and frequently purchased products.
- Evaluate discount usage and shipping preferences.
- Segment customers based on purchase history.
- Recommend data-driven strategies to improve customer loyalty and marketing performance.

## 5. Project Workflow

### Step 1: Data Loading and Initial Exploration

Loaded the dataset into Python using Pandas and examined its structure, data types, and summary statistics.

Key activities:
- Imported the dataset.
- Checked the number of rows and columns.
- Used `df.info()` to inspect data types and non-null values.
- Used `df.describe()` to understand numerical distributions.
- Identified missing values and potential data quality issues.

### Step 2: Data Cleaning and Feature Engineering

Prepared the dataset for reliable analysis by improving data consistency and creating useful analytical features.

Key activities:
- Checked missing values in the `review_rating` column.
- Imputed missing ratings using the median rating for the corresponding product category.
- Standardized column names using snake_case.
- Created an `age_group` column to compare customer segments.
- Created a `purchase_frequency_days` column for purchase frequency analysis.
- Checked the relationship between discount usage and promotional code usage.
- Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column, as documented in the project.

### Step 3: SQL Analysis

Loaded the prepared data into a relational database and used SQL queries to answer business questions.

The analysis covered:

1. **Revenue by Gender:** Compared revenue generated by male and female customers.
2. **High-Spending Discount Users:** Identified customers who used discounts but spent above the average purchase amount.
3. **Top-Rated Products:** Found the five products with the highest average review ratings.
4. **Shipping Analysis:** Compared average purchase amounts for Standard and Express shipping.
5. **Subscription Analysis:** Compared average spending and total revenue between subscribers and non-subscribers.
6. **Discount-Dependent Products:** Identified five products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases.
7. **Customer Segmentation:** Classified customers as New, Returning, or Loyal based on purchase history.
8. **Top Products by Category:** Identified the three most purchased products in each category.
9. **Repeat Purchases and Subscriptions:** Examined whether customers with more than five purchases were more likely to subscribe.
10. **Revenue by Age Group:** Calculated revenue contribution across customer age groups.

### Step 4: Power BI Dashboard

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate shopping behavior insights in a clear and accessible format.

The dashboard brings together customer spending, product preferences, subscription behavior, and purchasing trends to support business analysis.

Key analytical areas include:
- Customer demographics and purchasing patterns.
- Revenue and average spending comparisons.
- Product performance and review ratings.
- Subscription and discount behavior.
- Shipping preferences and customer segmentation.

## 6. Key Results and Business Recommendations

The analysis provides a structured way to understand customer shopping behavior and identify potential business improvement opportunities.

### Business Recommendations

**1. Increase Subscription Adoption**

Promote exclusive benefits, personalized offers, and subscriber rewards to encourage customers to join the subscription program.

**2. Strengthen Customer Loyalty**

Introduce loyalty programs and repeat-purchase incentives to encourage returning customers to become loyal customers.

**3. Optimize Discount Strategies**

Evaluate products with high discount dependency and balance promotional campaigns with profitability objectives.

**4. Improve Product Marketing**

Highlight highly rated and frequently purchased products in marketing campaigns and product recommendations.

**5. Use Targeted Marketing**

Focus campaigns on age groups and customer segments that contribute strongly to revenue, using the actual analysis results to guide targeting.

**6. Evaluate Shipping Preferences**

Analyze spending patterns across shipping methods to understand whether shipping preferences can inform customer experience and marketing strategies.

If the data source or file location has changed, update the connection settings and refresh the dataset.

Open the project report and Gamma presentation to review the methodology, business questions, dashboard insights, and recommendations.
